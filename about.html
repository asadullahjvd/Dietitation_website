About Dr. Sania

Board-certified nutritionist and dietitian, dedicated to evidence-based, food-first care.

Home / About

Dr. Sania is a board-certified nutritionist and dietitian with over 5 years of clinical experience. She holds a Doctorate in Nutritional Sciences from COMSATS University and has done MSc from Agriculture University.

Dr. Sania, a certified Consultant Dietitian and proud member of the Pakistan Nutrition Society (PNS), offering expert nutrition therapy for a wide range of health issues. With experience at CMH Okara, she specializes in treating conditions like obesity, diabetes, fatty liver, blood pressure, migraines, joint pain, stomach problems, and nutritional care during pregnancy—all through personalized diet plans. Whether you're dealing with PCOS or other chronic illnesses, Dr. Tania provides holistic, food-based solutions to help you regain your health naturally.

Credentials:

  • BS in Food Science and Nutrition from COMSATS University
  • MSc In HND from Agriculture University, Faisalabad
